## Decisions & Rationale
- **Multi-Cloud Template Parity**: Updated `isaac-workstation.pkr.hcl` across GCP, AWS, and Azure simultaneously, ensuring that `demos`, `install_gr00t`, and `enable_neo4j` can be flexibly pre-baked into custom golden images or configured at runtime.
- **Neo4j Self-Contained Handlers**: Created `src/ansible/roles/neo4j/handlers/main.yml` (`listen: reload_systemd`) and wired it into `isaac-workstation.yaml`, ensuring that `roles/neo4j` executes safely even when `roles/gr00t` is disabled.
- **Pre-Pulling Container Layers**: Neo4j 5.26 Community and Docker service configurations are pre-staged during Packer image builds, eliminating internet download latency during fresh workstation boots.
- **CLI Passthrough**: Updated `image-gcp`, `image-aws`, and `image-azure` to dynamically pass `--demos`, `--install-gr00t`, and `--enable-neo4j` to Packer's `-var` arguments.
